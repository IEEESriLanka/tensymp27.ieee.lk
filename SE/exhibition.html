Call for Exhibits

Call for Exhibits

IEEE R10 TENSYMP 2027 invites students from leading universities across Sri Lanka to showcase high-quality projects, prototypes, and demonstrations aligned with the conference theme. The exhibition will be held from 12–14 May 2027 at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya, alongside an expected 500 attendees from around 30 countries.

Exhibits will be evaluated by a panel of academics from leading universities. All selected exhibitors will receive a certificate, and the best three exhibits will receive cash prizes.

Who Should Exhibit

  • University students across Sri Lanka
  • Projects, prototypes, and demonstrations
  • Work relevant to the conference theme
  • Demonstrable products are preferred

Why Exhibit

  • Showcase your work to international delegates, researchers, and industry leaders
  • Certificate for all selected exhibitors
  • Cash prizes for the best three exhibits
  • Connect with experts from around the world

What to Submit

  • A 2-minute video presentation about your exhibit
  • Real information and images
  • Demonstrable products are preferred
  • Exhibits selected by a panel of academics

Exhibition Details

Theme

Connected Intelligence for Resilient Societies

Location

Exhibition Hall, Faculty of Engineering
University of Peradeniya
Sri Lanka

Evaluation Criteria

  • Technical maturity
  • Completeness of solution
  • Innovation & originality
  • Relevance to IEEE themes
  • Real-world impact & applicability
  • Presentation & team readiness
